---
title: Contact
---
To contact the AGN Science Collaboration, please use one of the forms:

- [Project Join Request](/project_join_request/) – To request to join an ongoing project.
- [Publication Review](/publication_review/) – To initiate the internal publication review process before journal submission.
- [Publication Dispute](/publication_dispute/) – To submit a dispute or clarification regarding authorship or publication matters.
- [General Inquiry](/general_inquiry/) – For any other questions or information requests.

Each submission will be directed to the appropriate team for prompt review and follow-up.
