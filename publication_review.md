Before you submit an article to a journal, you need to submit the Publication Review Form, in accordance with the [LSST AGN Science Collaboration Publication Policy](/sites/default/files/LSST_AGN_SC_Publication_Policy_202501_v3.pdf/). Submissions are reviewed by the AGN SC Publications Committee.

{% include form-embed.html %}

Each submission will be directed to the appropriate team for prompt review and follow-up.
