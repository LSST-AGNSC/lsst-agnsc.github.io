This form provides a formal mechanism for AGN SC members to initiate the internal publication review process, including:

- Ensuring that the publication has been appropriately circulated to relevant AGN SC groups.
- Confirming that authorship follows AGN SC guidelines and includes all eligible contributors.
- Identifying any scientific, procedural, or collaborative issues that may need attention prior to submission.

Once this form is submitted, the Publication Panel will review the materials and verify that the publication meets AGN SC expectations. If needed, the panel may provide feedback, request clarifications, or escalate concerns to subgroup coordinators or the AGN SC co-chairs.

We encourage lead authors to submit this form once the manuscript is ready for community review, allowing time for feedback and any necessary revisions before final submission
